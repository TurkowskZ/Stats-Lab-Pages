Formula Reference Guide

Sample Mean

$$\bar{X} = \frac{\sum X}{n}$$

Sum of Squared Deviations (SS)

$$\text{SS} = \sum (X_i - \bar{X})^2$$

Sample Variance

$$s^2 = \frac{\text{SS}}{n - 1}$$

Sample Standard Deviation

$$s = \sqrt{s^2}$$

Standard Error of the Mean

$$s_{\bar{X}} = \frac{s}{\sqrt{n}} = \sqrt{\frac{s^2}{n}}$$

Degrees of Freedom

$$\text{df} = n - 1$$

One Sample t Statistic

$$t = \frac{\bar{X} - \mu}{s_{\bar{X}}} = \frac{\bar{X} - \mu}{\sqrt{s^2 / n}}$$

Confidence Interval

$$\text{CI}_{1-\alpha} = \bar{X} \pm t_{\text{crit}} \cdot s_{\bar{X}}$$

Cohen's d (Effect Size)

$$d = \frac{\bar{X} - \mu}{s}$$
