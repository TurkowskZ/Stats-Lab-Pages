Stage 1 of 4

Group Means and Grand Mean

Formula for grand mean: $\bar{X}_{..} = \frac{\sum \sum X_{ij}}{N}$ where $N = n_1 + n_2 + n_3$.
Stage 2 of 4

Partitioning Sum of Squares

$SS_{treatment} = \sum n_j (\bar{X}_j - \bar{X}_{..})^2$

$SS_{error} = \sum \sum (X_{ij} - \bar{X}_j)^2$

$SS_{total} = \sum \sum (X_{ij} - \bar{X}_{..})^2 = SS_{treatment} + SS_{error}$

Stage 3 of 4

Degrees of Freedom and Mean Squares

$df_{treatment} = k - 1$ where $k = 3$

$df_{error} = N - k$ where $N = 21$

$MS_{treatment} = \frac{SS_{treatment}}{df_{treatment}}$ and $MS_{error} = \frac{SS_{error}}{df_{error}}$

Stage 4 of 4

Omnibus Test Statistic, Decision, and Effect Sizes

$F = \frac{MS_{treatment}}{MS_{error}}$

Eta squared: $\eta^2 = \frac{SS_{treatment}}{SS_{total}}$

Cohen $d$: $d = \sqrt{\frac{1}{df_{treatment}} \cdot \frac{\sum (\bar{X}_j - \bar{X}_{..})^2}{MS_{error}}}$
