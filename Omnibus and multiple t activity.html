Stage 1 of 4

Sample Means ($M_i$) and Grand Mean ($M_{\text{Grand}}$)

Formula for Grand Mean: $M_{\text{Grand}} = \frac{\sum^{scores} X}{n}$ where total sample size $n = n_1 + n_2 + n_3 = 21$.
Stage 2 of 4

Partitioning Sums of Squares ($SS$)

$SS_{Effect} \text{ (or } SS_{Between}\text{)} = \sum^{k} n_i (M_i - M_{\text{Grand}})^2$

$SS_{Error} \text{ (or } SS_{Within}\text{)} = \sum^{all\ scores} (X - M)^2$

$SS_{Total} = \sum^{scores} (X - M_{\text{Grand}})^2 = SS_{Effect} + SS_{Error}$

Stage 3 of 4

Degrees of Freedom ($df$) and Mean Squares ($s^2$ or $MS$)

$df_{Effect} \text{ (or } df_{Between}\text{)} = k - 1$ where $k = 3$

$df_{Error} \text{ (or } df_{Within}\text{)} = n - k$ where total $n = 21$ (and $df_{Total} = n - 1 = 20$)

$MS_{Effect} = SS_{Effect} / df_{Effect}$ and $MS_{Error} = SS_{Error} / df_{Error}$

Stage 4 of 4

Omnibus $F$, $F_{critical}$, Decision, and Effect Sizes

$F = \frac{MS_{Effect}}{MS_{Error}} = \frac{MS_{Between}}{MS_{Within}}$

Eta squared ($r$ family): $\eta^2 = \frac{SS_{between}}{SS_{total}} = \frac{MS_{between} * df_{between}}{(MS_{between} * df_{between}) + (MS_{within} * df_{within})}$

Standardized effect size ($d$ family): $d = \sqrt{\frac{1}{df_{Effect}} \cdot \frac{\sum (M_i - M_{\text{Grand}})^2}{MS_{Error}}}$
